02 · Research

Evolution of cratonic basins

past

Cratonic basins — broad, shallow depressions on ancient stable continental interiors — accumulate kilometres of sediment over geological time, faithfully recording the thermal and convective history of the mantle beneath them. This project, rooted in my undergraduate dissertation on the Parnaíba Basin, applied subsidence analysis and stratigraphic backstripping to constrain the long-term tectonic and mantle evolution of South American and North American cratonic interiors from the Cretaceous to the present.
