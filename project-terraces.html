02 · Research

Terraces as climate archives

current

River terraces are nature’s own stratigraphic archive, preserving former flood levels as successive staircase-like benches, nested within valley walls. I use cosmogenic 10Be dating of terraces of the only river system in Patagonia that links the Patagonian Ice Field to the Atlantic Ocean: the Shehuén-Santa Cruz river system.
