About

Geomorphologist and Assistant Lecturer at Monash University. I combine field mapping, cosmogenic nuclide dating, low-temperature thermochronology and landscape-evolution modelling to read how climate and deep-Earth forces shape continents — from the rivers of Patagonia to the terraces of Tasmania. PhD from Imperial College London, with postdoctoral research at GFZ Potsdam and Imperial.
